About Privacy Support

An offline-first reading companion for children and families, with accessible display controls, read-aloud support, fluency practice, comprehension activities, and a private parent-facing progress view.

Five reading languages

Turkish, Spanish, English, Brazilian Portuguese, and Swedish.

Private by design

No account, ads, analytics, tracking, or cloud profile.

Accessible reading

Adjustable type, spacing, themes, highlighting, and device voices.

Use your own text

Import supported documents or a secure web page for local reading.

Educational support, not diagnosis

The app supports reading practice and family routines. It is not a medical device, diagnostic test, treatment, or replacement for professional educational or clinical advice.
