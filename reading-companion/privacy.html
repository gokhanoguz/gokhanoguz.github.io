Short version: reader profiles, imported text, and progress are stored on the device. The app has no reading account, cloud sync, ads, or behavioral tracking. Optional Plus billing uses RevenueCat and your app store for purchase records and subscription checks, only after a parent connects billing or restores purchases. Optional read-aloud may send the text being spoken, including imported text, to the selected voice provider for online processing. Optional read-along uses the device's speech-recognition service, which may process microphone audio online, while the reader is actively reading. The app never saves voice audio or the temporary transcript.

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally so its reading features work:

  • reader name, optional age band, reading language, and start level;
  • reading sessions, drill results, comprehension progress, and words to revisit;
  • display, theme, voice, and read-along preferences; and
  • text or documents that a parent or caregiver chooses to import.

This data is not uploaded to us. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, diagnosis, or comparison with other readers.

2. Accounts and cloud services

The app does not require an account or sign-in. There is no cloud profile, cloud backup, public profile, leaderboard, chat, or social feature.

In versions with Plus billing enabled, a parent can connect RevenueCat and the device's app store from the parent-gated Plus screen. The app explains this connection before the parent chooses to view plans or restore purchases. Free reading does not require this connection. Earlier builds without configured purchases do not connect to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat processes an anonymous app purchase identifier, store transaction and subscription details, entitlement status, and technical app/store information needed to provide billing. This supports purchase verification, restoration, subscription access and purchase analytics. We can view these purchase records in RevenueCat's dashboard. We do not send reader names, ages, reading languages, imported content, reading progress, voice audio or transcripts to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat also processes country-level information from store transactions and may infer and retain country from your connection’s IP address for subscription analytics. The app does not request GPS or device-location permissions.

After a parent connects billing, the app may check subscription status on future launches and resumes. A bounded local cache supports temporary offline access. RevenueCat's optional diagnostic reporting, automatic advertising-identifier collection and attribution integrations are not enabled by this app. Store payment information is handled by Apple or Google; we do not receive full payment-card details.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

Read-along is optional and off by default. If a parent enables it and the reader starts reading aloud, the app asks the operating system's speech-recognition service to create a temporary text transcript. The transcript is used in memory to highlight progress through the passage.

The app does not access or save raw voice audio, does not save the transcript, and does not send either to a server operated by us. Depending on the device, installed language, and operating-system settings, Apple, Google, or another configured speech provider may process speech on the device or through its own servers. That processing is governed by the provider's privacy terms. Microphone permission can be revoked at any time in device settings.

4. Text-to-speech

Read-aloud passes the text being spoken to your device's selected text-to-speech service. Some voices process text on the device; network-based voices may send that text, including text from imported documents, to their provider's servers under that provider's privacy terms. We do not operate a text-to-speech server.

5. Imported files and web pages

A parent or caregiver can choose a supported document from the device or share one from another app. The app parses that file locally and stores the resulting reading text locally.

If a parent imports a web page, the app makes a direct encrypted HTTPS request from the device to the website selected by the parent. The chosen website will receive ordinary connection information such as the device's IP address and the requested URL under that website's own privacy policy. We do not proxy, log, or receive the URL or page content. The extracted reading text is stored locally. Optional read-aloud processing is described in section 4 above.

6. Exports and sharing

A parent can explicitly export a progress report as PDF or CSV through the operating system's share sheet. The app sends the report only to the destination the parent chooses. After sharing, that destination's privacy practices apply.

7. Third-party and platform services

  • Apple or Google speech recognition, or another speech service configured on the device, for optional live read-along.
  • Apple or Android text-to-speech, or an installed device voice, for reading text aloud.
  • The website selected by a parent when importing a secure web page.
  • The destination selected by a parent when exporting or sharing a report.
  • RevenueCat and Apple App Store or Google Play for optional parent-authorized billing. See RevenueCat's privacy policy, Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

We do not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob, Facebook SDK, advertising identifiers, attribution SDKs, or third-party behavioral analytics. The app contains no ads.

8. Children

The app is designed for children and families. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children on a developer-operated server. There is no account, public communication, advertising, or behavioral tracking. Parent-facing management, imports, exports, and progress controls are placed in the grown-ups area.

Optional subscriptions are for parents and caregivers. Anonymous purchase records are not linked by the app to a child's local reader profile and are not used for advertising or behavioral tracking.

9. Deleting data

A parent can delete reader profiles and imported texts inside the app. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage through device settings, removes all app-managed local data. Because we do not hold a remote reader record, there is no developer-held cloud reading history to delete. Purchase records are separate: uninstalling does not cancel a subscription or delete RevenueCat or store records. A parent can request deletion of developer-controlled purchase data by email, using the anonymous purchase support ID available in the Plus screen. See purchase-data deletion.

10. Data retention and security

Local information remains until a parent deletes it, clears app storage, or uninstalls the app. Purchase records may remain with RevenueCat and the store to provide subscriptions, handle refunds, prevent fraud and meet legal financial-record requirements. We review deletion requests and remove developer-controlled records that no longer need to be kept; any applicable retention limits will be explained in our response. Parent-requested web imports and billing connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep the device protected and report suspected vulnerabilities to the contact address below.

11. Your rights and contact

For privacy questions or rights requests, email m.gokhanoguz2@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. Local reader data can normally be accessed, corrected or deleted directly on the device. For purchase records or support correspondence, contact us; we may ask for limited information to locate and verify the record. Do not send a child's name, reading content, passwords or payment-card details. Email correspondence is processed to respond to your request and retained only as needed for support, dispute resolution or applicable legal obligations.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices materially change, this page and its "Last updated" date will be revised before the changed version is submitted to an app store.
