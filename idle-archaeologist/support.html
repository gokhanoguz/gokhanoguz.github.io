How the game works

What is Idle Archaeologist?

An idle museum-curation game. Your dig sites unearth artifacts; your workshop restores them; your patrons fund the next expedition. Single player, plays offline, runs on iOS and Android.

Eras & prestige

When you've earned enough renown you can Donate your collection to the museum, prestige, and unlock the next historical era. Each era brings new dig sites, patrons, and artifacts. Your gold and artifacts reset, but gems, renown, and era progress carry forward.

Workshop restoration

Artifacts arrive damaged and must be restored in the workshop before they can be displayed. Restoration takes real time (or gems / a short ad to skip). Rarer artifacts take longer but are worth more renown and patron interest.

Patron contracts

Patrons appear with time-limited contracts offering gold, gems, or both. Curator commissions are permanent standing offers that never expire.

Purchases & subscriptions

Patron Pass

An optional monthly subscription that grants 50 gems per daily login, removes interstitial ads, and doubles patron tip rewards. It auto-renews until you cancel. Manage it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Gem packs & Quiet Museum

Gem packs are one-time consumable purchases. The Quiet Museum (remove interstitial ads) is a one-time non-consumable purchase. Rewarded ads remain available even after purchasing Quiet Museum.

Restore purchases

If you reinstall the app or switch devices, open Settings → Shop and tap Restore Purchases to recover non-consumable and subscription entitlements.

Save data & privacy

Idle Archaeologist stores all your progress locally on your device. We do not keep a copy of your save data on any server and cannot recover it if you uninstall the app. For details on how the app handles data, see the Privacy Policy.

What to include in a bug report

To help us fix things faster, please include:

  • Your device model (e.g., iPhone 15, Pixel 8) and OS version.
  • The app version (Settings → About).
  • What you were doing when the issue happened.
  • What you expected vs. what actually happened.
  • A screenshot or short screen recording, if possible.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) and Google (Play Store). Refund requests must be made through their respective support channels:

  • Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google: Google Play refunds help
