Block Shift is an untimed block puzzle for Android, with an iOS version in preparation. Place shapes on an 8×8 board, clear lines, and use one optional row or column shift per tray to make room.

Current Android release: no ads or purchase offers. Availability depends on Google Play review and rollout status. Check Google Play availability; the listing may not be accessible until release approval. There is no public iOS release yet.

A puzzle without the hurry

  • Drag shapes, or tap a shape and then a board cell.
  • Hold the board, slide a row or column, and release to shift it one cell.
  • Play a dated Daily Shift with free retries, or try multi-tray challenges.
  • Learn with guided practice and short Starter puzzles.
  • No gameplay timer, lives, coin economy or developer-operated game account.

Your game stays yours

Game progress and settings are stored locally. Core puzzles work offline, but Android build 28 may require a Google Play verification check, and the iOS build 30 candidate uses Apple's system age checks. A known requirement or an unreadable saved check may need recovery before play can continue. An unavailable check does not otherwise stop existing puzzles. Older test advertising and purchase services need a connection. There is no developer-operated game-progress sync service. See Support for the version-specific behavior.

No ads or purchase offers in this release

Gameplay, hints and retries do not require a purchase. Any future advertising or purchase features will be described before they are introduced; this is not a promise that every future version will be ad-free. Older private billing-test builds are separate and are explained in Support.
