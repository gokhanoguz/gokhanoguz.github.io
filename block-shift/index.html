Block Shift is a small, offline-first puzzle for Android and iOS. Place shapes on an 8×8 board, clear lines, and use one optional row or column shift per tray to make room.

In development — private testing. Block Shift is not publicly available from the app stores yet. This page provides information and support for the current test builds.

A puzzle without the hurry

  • Drag shapes, or tap a shape and then a board cell.
  • Hold the board, slide a row or column, and release to shift it one cell.
  • Play a dated Daily Shift with free retries, or try multi-tray challenges.
  • Learn with guided practice and short Starter puzzles.
  • No gameplay timer, lives, coin economy or required account.

Your game stays yours

Game progress and settings are stored locally. Core play works offline; test advertising and purchase services need a connection. There is no developer-operated game-progress sync service.

Testing monetization carefully

The proposed model is restrained ads between games and one optional, non-subscription No Ads purchase. Existing gameplay, hints and retries remain free. Current local-demo and RevenueCat Test Store purchases are simulations, not real purchases. Google Play billing tests require a designated license-test account and Google's test payment methods.
