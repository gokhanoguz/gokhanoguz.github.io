How the game works

What is a cipher run?

Each run uses one randomly-generated substitution alphabet. You see a series of inscriptions encoded with that alphabet, starting easy and ending with a famous-quote finale. Decoding any letter in one inscription decodes it everywhere, so the run gets easier as you progress.

Daily vs endless runs

The daily run uses a seed that's the same for every player on a given UTC day. Endless runs use a fresh random seed and don't affect your streak — play as many as you like.

Streaks

Completing the daily run on a given UTC day extends your streak. Missing a day breaks it. If life gets in the way, a free streak rescue is available roughly once every 30 days. Version 1.1.0 also includes one weekly streak shield for everyone. Rescues and shields are offered when you return after missing exactly one day.

Hints

Hints reveal one letter mapping at the cost of points. The cost and number of available hints depend on difficulty.

Account & data

Do I need to sign in?

No. Version 1.1.0 removes accounts, cloud sync, and leaderboards entirely. All game features are free and progress stays on-device. Older installed versions may still show sign-in and purchase options; update in place rather than uninstalling to preserve local progress.

How do I delete my account?

For an account created in an older version, email m.gokhanoguz2@gmail.com. Include the sign-in provider or email used, but never your password. We can help with access, export, and deletion of legacy cloud records, normally within 30 days. Updating the app does not itself delete them. See the privacy policy for details.

What happened to Pro?

In version 1.1.0 every former Pro feature is free: Expert difficulty, palettes, past dailies, detailed stats, and the weekly shield. Existing purchasers keep access without signing in or restoring a purchase. Historical payment or refund questions should be directed to the store where the purchase was made.

I deleted the app — is my progress gone?

Local progress (run history, settings, achievements) is removed with the app. Version 1.1.0 has no cloud restore or cross-device sync. Your device's backup settings may affect recovery. Contact support for access to legacy cloud records, but do not assume they can restore an offline save.

Common issues

Where are the leaderboards?

Public leaderboards are removed in version 1.1.0. Your local scores, history, achievements, and daily streaks remain available in Profile. Old queued scores are not uploaded by the offline update.

Does this update address the Firestore SDK notice?

Version 1.1.0 removes Firebase and Firestore SDKs from the app. Previously installed versions are unchanged until you install the update. Update from your app store when it becomes available.

The puzzle has no solution / the answer doesn't match

Each puzzle is generated deterministically and is fully solvable. If you believe you've found a counter-example, please email us with the seed (visible on the run results screen) and a screenshot — that's enough information to reproduce the run exactly.

I want to suggest a quote / inscription / language

Suggestions are welcome. Email m.gokhanoguz2@gmail.com with the text and a source if applicable.

Bug reports

The most useful bug reports include:

  • Device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18.3)
  • App version (from your device's app information)
  • What you did, what you expected, what happened
  • If reproducible: the run seed and difficulty
  • Screenshot or screen recording, if relevant
