Version 1.1.0 and later are free, offline games. They do not create accounts or send gameplay data to us. There are no ads, analytics, tracking SDKs, cloud sync, leaderboards, or in-app purchases. Older installed versions and previously stored cloud data are described separately below. Store availability may vary during rollout.

1. Data on your device

Puzzle saves, settings, run history, XP, achievements, daily results, streaks, and personal statistics are stored locally. The app does not upload them. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings. We cannot restore local progress from our servers in version 1.1.0 or later.

Updating in place preserves compatible local data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage can erase it. No data migration to the cloud takes place when you update.

2. Optional sharing, reminders, and links

Sharing a result opens your device's share sheet. You choose the recipient or app; the selected service handles what you share under its own policies. Reminders are optional local notifications and do not use a remote push service. You can turn them off in Profile or your device settings.

Privacy, terms, and support links open your browser. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; its provider may process standard request information such as IP addresses for delivery and security. The game does not send your saved progress when opening a link.

3. Older versions and existing cloud accounts

Versions before 1.1.0 use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google). They can create an anonymous account and offer Apple or Google sign-in. Account information may include a Firebase identifier, provider identifiers, display name, email or Apple relay address, and profile image information supplied by the provider. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.

Older versions can upload progress, scores, language, difficulty, timestamps, accuracy, run duration, achievements, and streak state. Display names and leaderboard scores may be visible to other players. Legacy purchase validation uses store transaction or receipt information and an account identifier to check purchases with Apple or Google. Store providers also process purchases under their own privacy policies.

Updating does not automatically delete cloud accounts or previously uploaded data. Existing records remain until deleted or retired through a separately managed process. Older installed versions may continue to contact these services. Update to the latest version to stop the app's Firebase activity; deleting cloud data is a separate request.

4. Access, export, and deletion

To request access, correction, export, or deletion of a legacy account and associated cloud progress, email m.gokhanoguz2@gmail.com. Mention Forgotten Script and the sign-in provider or account email used. Do not send passwords, payment-card information, or identity documents. We may ask for proportionate verification of account ownership. We respond within 30 days.

Version 1.1.0 has no account screen, so email remains available even after updating or uninstalling. A cloud deletion request covers the authentication record and associated private progress and public leaderboard entries we control. Any information that must be retained for legal or transaction-record obligations will be explained in the response. Apple and Google handle their own store records.

Local data can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling; device backups are managed separately in your operating system. This may permanently erase progress. We cannot remotely access or delete the offline database on your device.

5. Retention and service providers

Local progress remains until removed from the device. Legacy cloud records are retained while the old service remains in operation or until an applicable deletion request is completed. Support email is used to resolve your request, not for advertising. We do not sell personal information.

Legacy Firebase services are operated by Google and may process data in countries where Google maintains infrastructure. Access is controlled by authentication and database rules; public leaderboard information is intentionally shared. No system can guarantee complete security. Please report concerns to the contact address above.

6. Children

The offline game does not collect children's personal information. If you believe an older version or a support request has provided personal data about a child, contact us to request its removal.

7. Policy changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected in the date and text on this page. Questions or requests: m.gokhanoguz2@gmail.com.
