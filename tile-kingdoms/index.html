About

Tile Kingdoms is a multiplayer hex-grid strategy game that runs in 4-week seasons. Found a kingdom, gather resources, train armies, and battle real players on a shared world map. At the end of each season, the slate is wiped — but your legacy lives on in the Hall of Fame.

No pay-to-win. No forced ads. Free to play, with an optional Season Pass and cosmetic shop.
